1 Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Dragoster Studios ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the Indian Ninja Frog: Platformer mobile game application ("the App") available on Google Play.

The App does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or contact details. To support the App and keep it free to play, the App displays advertisements through Google AdMob, which involves limited, standard data use described below.

🛡️ Short version: No account required. No personal information like your name or email is collected. The App shows ads through Google AdMob to stay free, which uses your device's advertising ID as described in Section 3 and 4 below. Your game progress is saved locally on your device.

2 Data Collection

Indian Ninja Frog: Platformer does not require account creation and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or physical location.

Because the App displays advertisements through Google AdMob, your device's advertising ID is accessed and shared with Google for the purpose of showing and measuring ads. This is the only identifier collected by the App. See Sections 3 and 4 for details.

✕ No personal information ✕ No email addresses ✕ No location data ✕ No account required ✕ No purchase tracking ✓ Advertising ID (for ads only)
3 Third-Party Services

This App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is a service provided by Google that may collect and process certain data — including your device's advertising ID — to serve and measure ads. This data is handled by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and their how Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services.

We do not use any other third-party analytics, crash reporting, or social media SDKs beyond Google AdMob.

✓ Google AdMob (advertising) ✕ No Google Analytics ✕ No Firebase Analytics ✕ No Facebook SDK ✕ No other third-party SDKs
4 Advertisements

Indian Ninja Frog: Platformer displays advertisements through Google AdMob to support free access to the game. Ads may appear in the following formats:

🖼️ Banner ads (during gameplay) 🎬 Interstitial ads (between levels / after certain events) 🎁 Rewarded ads (optional, watched voluntarily for in-game rewards)

The App declares and uses the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission so that Google AdMob can access the device's advertising ID to serve and measure these ads. You can reset or limit use of your advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

5 Children's Privacy

This App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or contact details from anyone, including children.

Because the App serves ads through Google AdMob using the advertising ID, it is not specifically designed for or directed at children under 13. Parents and guardians of younger players are encouraged to supervise app usage and review Google's own privacy practices for advertising.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

6 Data Security

We do not operate a server that stores personal data — your game progress and settings are saved locally on your device only and are never transmitted to us. The only data leaving your device is the advertising ID shared with Google AdMob for ad delivery, handled under Google's own security and privacy practices.

7 Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
